Proposed Heritage Overlays in Balwyn North and Surrey Hills

By Sonny Williamson • July 30, 2026
Heated discussion about this proposal across the neighborhood.

A new local heritage proposal in the leafy suburb of Surrey Hills has ignited intense discussion among residents, property owners, and urban planning experts.

Under recent planning scheme amendments pushed by the local council (City of Boroondara), several additional residential pockets and commercial strips across Surrey Hills featuring Victorian, Edwardian, and California Bungalow architecture are being considered for inclusion in the formal Heritage Overlay (HO).

The Core of the Proposal: Safeguarding Nearly 180 Years of Architecture

Surrey Hills has long been revered for its tree-lined streetscapes and well-preserved historic homes. Council planners argue that expanding heritage protections is vital for maintaining the suburb's character and preventing unsympathetic redevelopments or full demolitions.

"Surrey Hills derives its charm from its century-old homes and unique neighbourhood feel. If we allow developers to demolish these historic properties unchecked, the suburb loses its identity forever. Heritage overlays ensure we preserve history for future generations." — Sarah M., a local resident of 20 years.

As the Victorian state government continues to push for higher-density housing targets near transport hubs, the tension between local council heritage overlays and state-level housing goals remains at an all-time high. The Council's public consultation process gives affected landholders and residents the opportunity to submit formal feedback. An independent panel hearing will review all submissions before submitting a final recommendation report to the Minister for Planning.
