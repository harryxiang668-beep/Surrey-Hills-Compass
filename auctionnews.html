Bidding War! 1 Bonavista Ave Fetches $3,815,000 in Tense Auction

By RT James • November 1, 2025
Property overview & walkthrough: 1 Bona Vista Avenue, Surrey Hills.

A fierce bidding war erupted in one of Surrey Hills' most coveted pockets over the weekend, as a pristine residential property at 1 Bonavista Avenue sold under the hammer for a impressive $3,815,000 after dozens of competitive offers.

The highly anticipated auction drew a crowd of nearly 100 spectators, curious neighbours, and serious bidders spilling onto the tree-lined street. Bidding opened briskly, but quickly escalated into a tense, high-stakes showdown between multiple determined buyers who refused to back down.

Over a Dozen Rapid Bids Push Price Well Past Expectations

With the auctioneer orchestrating a fast-paced environment, counter-offers flew in rapid succession. More than a dozen intense rounds of bidding pushed the final figure well beyond the initial reserve price, keeping onlookers captivated until the very last moment.

"The energy on the street was electric. Properties along Bonavista Avenue are notoriously tightly held, and when one comes to market, buyers are prepared to fight hard for it." — A local real estate analyst present at the auction.

The winning bid came from a buyer of Chinese background, who stood firm through the final flurry of incremental bids to secure the keys. The landmark sale at $3,815,000 highlights the continued strength and unwavering demand for prime family residences in the leafy heart of Surrey Hills.
